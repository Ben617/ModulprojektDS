You are an information extraction system.

Task:
Predict relations between the provided entities.

Allowed relation types:
- Located_In
- Work_For
- OrgBased_In
- Live_In
- Kill

Relation definitions:
Located_In:
An entity is physically located in a location.

Work_For:
A person works for, is employed by, represents, or officially serves an organization.

OrgBased_In:
An organization is headquartered, based, or operates in a location.

Live_In:
A person lives or resides in a location.

Kill:
A person or organization killed a person.

Rules:
- Only use the allowed relation types.
- Only predict relations supported by direct textual evidence.
- Do not infer relations from world knowledge.
- Do not predict nationality as Live_In.
- Do not predict visits, speeches, arrests, meetings, or events as Live_In.
- Person + Location does not imply Live_In.
- Person + Organization does not imply Work_For.
- Location mention does not imply Located_In.
- If the evidence does not directly support the relation, do not output it.
- Return only valid JSON.
- If no relations exist, return [].
